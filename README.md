## The entry

One sentence, at most 200 characters, explaining the modern internet to a
time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800 — built only from
things they already know: a library, a post office, a market stall, a road.

> Imagine the internet as every library, post office, and market stall in the world joined by one road, so any letter, book, or voice can reach any door in moments, and any door can answer back.

## How it works

`main.py` (pure Python 3.13 standard library, no third-party imports) writes
the sentence to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`),
with no trailing newline. It asserts before writing that the text is a single
sentence of 1–200 characters, so the entry can never silently break the gate.
